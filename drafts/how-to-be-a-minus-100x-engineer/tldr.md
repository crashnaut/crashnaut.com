title: "How to Be a -100x Engineer: Let the Agent Read Your Messages"
slug: how-to-be-a-minus-100x-engineer
---

- **The loop:** an agent reads Jira and Slack over MCP and posts the reply. No human reads either end.
- **The economics:** writing costs the sender seconds; every reader pays in full. Example: 10 posts a day of 600 words to 40 readers is about 100 team hours a week.
- **The filter:** ask what they did, why, and what the value was. If they can't answer in a minute, don't read the long version.

To be +1x:

1. Read the ticket yourself; use the agent to find related tickets and code.
2. Post the answer to the three questions, not the generated update.
3. Ask the agent to make your message shorter.
4. Give MCP servers read access by default; grant posting on purpose.
